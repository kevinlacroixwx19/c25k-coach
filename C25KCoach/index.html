About your plan

Three sessions each week, ideally with a rest day between. Every session includes a 5-minute warm-up walk and 5-minute cool-down walk. Run gently enough to speak in short sentences. Repeat a week if needed. Week 9 builds to 30 minutes of running, which may not equal 5 km for every runner.

Workout history is saved on this device. If you clear website data or change devices, progress may be lost.
